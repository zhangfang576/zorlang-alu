In May 2021 Yunnan began enforcing ordered power consumption, and aluminium smelters — the province's largest industrial electricity users — were among the first to be curtailed. The province had spent five years attracting greenfield smelting capacity on the promise of cheap hydropower. That spring, the water was not there.

## What happened

- **Hydropower output fell short.** Low reservoir levels ahead of the rainy season cut available generation, and the province prioritised residential and other industrial users over electrolysis.
- **Smelters reduced load rather than shutting down.** Many cut current across operating pots, which is technically easier than stopping a line but still costs efficiency and current efficiency.
- **New capacity was frozen.** Projects due to commission in 2021 were delayed, removing expected supply from the second half of the year.
- **The price kept rising.** LME aluminium moved from around USD 2,400/t in May toward USD 2,700/t by July, with the Yunnan story as the main supply argument.

## The structural problem nobody priced

Hydropower is seasonal. A province that builds 4–5 Mt of smelting capacity on hydro has, in a dry year, 20–30% less power than the nameplate assumes. Yunnan's aluminium capacity was designed around an average water year; the market had been pricing it as firm.

That mismatch is why Yunnan has now produced supply shocks in **2021, 2022 and again in the 2023–2024 dry season**, and why Chinese smelters increasingly treat provincial hydropower as interruptible.

## What it means for buyers

- **"Green aluminium" is not always firm aluminium.** Low-carbon hydro-based metal can carry a seasonal availability risk that coal-based metal does not. If you are contracting annual tonnage, ask whether the allocation survives a dry year.
- **Billet and extrusion supply tightens first.** When Yunnan cuts, local billet supply to surrounding extruders tightens within weeks, before sheet and plate show it.
- **Watch the reservoir, not the policy.** The earliest warning is hydrological data in April–May, roughly two months before the price reacts.
- **Qualify a second region.** Buyers with a single-province supply chain in China paid spot premiums in the second half of 2021 that a dual-region chain avoided.

## Where it points

Yunnan's 2021 curtailment was the second leg of the rally that peaked in October 2021 above USD 3,200/t. It also began the market's habit of separating **low-carbon metal** from **firm metal** — a distinction that became contractual once CBAM reporting started in 2023.
